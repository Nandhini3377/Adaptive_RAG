# Nimbus Labs Employee Handbook

Welcome to Nimbus Labs. This handbook explains how we work together, what you can expect from us, and what we expect from you. Please read it during your first week.

## Time off

Every employee receives 20 days of annual leave per year. Plan longer holidays with your team at least two weeks ahead so work can be shared fairly.

Employees also get 10 days of sick leave per year. If you are unwell, tell your manager as early as you can on the day.

In addition, there are 5 days of casual leave for short personal errands. Up to 5 unused days of annual leave carry over to the next year.

Parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into two parts within the first year.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, when everyone should be available for meetings. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as suits you best.

Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week. Agree your office days with your team so that meetings in person stay useful.

## Money and equipment

Expenses up to ₹5,000 can be claimed without manager approval. Larger expenses need written approval from your manager before you spend the money.

Laptops are replaced every 3 years, and you can request a new one through the help desk. Please return your old laptop within one week of receiving a new one.

Each employee has a learning budget of ₹40,000 per year for courses and books. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Joining and leaving

New employees have a probation period of 6 months. During probation your manager will meet you every month to discuss how things are going.

The notice period for resignation is 60 days. Your manager can agree to a shorter notice period when the handover is complete.
